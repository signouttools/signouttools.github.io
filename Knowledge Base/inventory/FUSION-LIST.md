# Oncogenic fusion candidate inventory

Compiled 2026-11-27. Planning only; no report blurbs.

**152 distinct ordered pair candidates**: wave 1: 71, wave 2: 70, wave 3: 11. **21 regulatory-event candidates** are listed separately.

This is a proposed curation list, not an exhaustive fusion database or an adjudicated list of oncogenic variants. A listed gene pair does not establish the significance of every breakpoint, transcript, reciprocal product, or tumor context. Source links are discovery leads, and pair-specific primary full-text evidence remains to be collected. Context labels are review targets, not approved diagnostic assertions.

Partners use local gene-dictionary symbols and IDs. External HGNC revalidation remains pending. Rollout waves are editorial priorities, not clinical evidence tiers.

Partners missing from the current 1,305-gene dictionary: **AHCYL1, BCL9, CARS1, DDX10, EBF1, EZR, FAM131B, HNRNPUL1, HOXD13, INA, LIFR, MLLT6, RCSD1, SDC4, SET, SQSTM1, SRGAP3, SSBP2, TPM4**. Add identity stubs only after HGNC review; no new gene paragraphs are part of this plan.
