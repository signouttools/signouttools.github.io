## EML4::ALK

**Tumor scope:** Lung adenocarcinoma.

EML4 encodes a microtubule-associated protein with an amino-terminal oligomerization domain. ([EML4 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/27436)). ALK encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase involved in neural development. ([ALK — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/238)). An intrachromosomal inversion joins the EML4 oligomerization region to the ALK kinase domain, producing ligand-independent kinase signaling and an established lung-cancer driver. ([EML4::ALK transforming fusion discovery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17625570/)). ALK-positive metastatic NSCLC is eligible for ALK-directed kinase inhibition; alectinib also has a US adjuvant indication after resection of ALK-positive NSCLC, with the pivotal trial enrolling stage IB tumors at least 4 cm through stage IIIA disease. ([FDA crizotinib label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/202570s033lbl.pdf), [FDA alectinib adjuvant approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-alectinib-adjuvant-treatment-alk-positive-non-small-cell-lung-cancer)).

## KIF5B::ALK

**Tumor scope:** Lung adenocarcinoma.

KIF5B encodes a kinesin motor protein that transports cargo along microtubules. ([KIF5B — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/3799)). ALK encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase involved in neural development. ([ALK — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/238)). The fusion retains the ALK kinase domain under an amino-terminal partner that promotes abnormal kinase activation. It is a rare driver in lung adenocarcinoma. A published lung-cancer case responded to crizotinib and later acquired ALK L1196M. ([KIF5B::ALK lung cancer response](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33832282/)). ALK-directed treatment is supported by the ALK-positive NSCLC indication; the cited evidence for this particular partner is a case report. ([FDA crizotinib label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/202570s033lbl.pdf), [KIF5B::ALK lung cancer response](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33832282/)).

## KIF5B::RET

**Tumor scope:** Lung adenocarcinoma.

KIF5B encodes a kinesin motor protein that transports cargo along microtubules. ([KIF5B — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/3799)). RET encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase activated by GDNF-family ligands and their coreceptors. ([RET — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/5979)). ## FGFR3::TACC3

**Tumor scope:** Glioblastoma; Urothelial carcinoma.

FGFR3 encodes a fibroblast growth factor receptor tyrosine kinase. ([FGFR3 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/2261)). TACC3 encodes a coiled-coil protein involved in mitotic-spindle organization. ([TACC3 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/10460)). The fusion retains the FGFR kinase domain and the TACC coiled-coil region, promoting constitutive kinase activity and abnormal mitotic-spindle function. Transforming activity has been demonstrated in glioma models. ([FGFR::TACC transforming fusions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3677224/)). FGFR inhibition has shown activity in experimental fusion-positive glioma models; this preclinical evidence does not establish an approved glioma indication. ([FGFR::TACC transforming fusions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3677224/)). In adults with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, a susceptible FGFR3 alteration such as FGFR3::TACC3 can support erdafitinib treatment after at least one prior systemic therapy. The US label does not recommend erdafitinib before PD-1/PD-L1 therapy in eligible patients. This indication does not cover glioma. ([FDA erdafitinib approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-erdafitinib-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-urothelial-carcinoma), [FDA erdafitinib label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/212018s007s008s009lbl.pdf)).

## GATA2 enhancer / MECOM

**Tumor scope:** AML with inv(3) or t(3;3).

**Event type:** regulatory rearrangement.

GATA2 encodes a zinc-finger transcription factor required for hematopoietic stem/progenitor-cell function. ([GATA2 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/2624)). ([GATA2 enhancer relocation and EVI1 deregulation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24703711/)). AML with inv(3)/t(3;3) is in the adverse genetic-risk group under adult ELN 2022 intensive-treatment criteria. ([ELN 2022 AML recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2022016867)).

## MALAT1::TFEB

**Tumor scope:** TFEB-rearranged renal cell carcinoma.

**Event type:** promoter substitution.

TFEB encodes a MiT/TFE-family transcription factor that regulates lysosome biogenesis and autophagy. ([TFEB — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/7942)). The rearrangement places TFEB under MALAT1 promoter control, increasing TFEB expression and lysosomal/metabolic transcription.
