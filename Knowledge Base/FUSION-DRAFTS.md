## EML4::ALK

**Tumor scope:** Lung adenocarcinoma.

**Evidence:** Primary fusion results checked.

EML4 encodes a microtubule-associated protein with an amino-terminal oligomerization domain. ([EML4 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/27436)). ALK encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase involved in neural development. ([ALK — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/238)). An inversion within chromosome 2 joins EML4 to the ALK kinase region. The fusion has kinase-dependent transforming activity: expressing it produced fibroblast foci and tumors in mice, whereas a kinase-inactive mutant did not. It was detected in non-small-cell lung cancers. ([EML4::ALK transforming fusion discovery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17625570/)). ALK-positive metastatic NSCLC is eligible for ALK-directed kinase inhibition; alectinib also has a US adjuvant indication after resection of ALK-positive NSCLC, with the pivotal trial enrolling stage IB tumors at least 4 cm through stage IIIA disease. ([FDA crizotinib label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/202570s033lbl.pdf), [FDA alectinib adjuvant approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-alectinib-adjuvant-treatment-alk-positive-non-small-cell-lung-cancer)).

## ETV6::NTRK2

**Tumor scope:** AML.

The patient also harbored ETV6::MECOM and other alterations, and the response differed between clones. This is case-level clinical evidence. ([Oncogenic TRK fusions are amenable to inhibition in hematologic malignancies](https://dm5migu4zj3pb.cloudfront.net/manuscripts/120000/120787/JCI120787.v3.pdf)).

## FGFR3::TACC3

**Tumor scope:** Glioblastoma; Urothelial carcinoma.

**Evidence:** Primary fusion results checked.

FGFR3 encodes a fibroblast growth factor receptor tyrosine kinase. ([FGFR3 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/2261)). TACC3 encodes a coiled-coil protein involved in mitotic-spindle organization. ([TACC3 — NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/10460)). The fusion retains the FGFR kinase domain and the TACC coiled-coil region, promoting constitutive kinase activity and abnormal mitotic-spindle function. Transforming activity has been demonstrated in glioma models. ([FGFR::TACC primary fusion experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3677224/)). In adults with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, a susceptible FGFR3 alteration such as FGFR3::TACC3 can support erdafitinib treatment after at least one prior systemic therapy. The US label does not recommend erdafitinib before PD-1/PD-L1 therapy in eligible patients. This indication does not cover glioma. ([FDA erdafitinib approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-erdafitinib-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-urothelial-carcinoma), [FDA erdafitinib label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/212018s007s008s009lbl.pdf)).

([Primary translocation renal carcinoma genomic cohort](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/170559/files/pdf)).
