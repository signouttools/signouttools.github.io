About this knowledge base

This library provides draft report comments about gene function, inherited disease, somatic alterations, and cancer associations. All comments require molecular pathologist review.

The original 1,133-gene roster combines versioned gene lists from MSK-IMPACT 505, MSK-IMPACT Heme, DFCI OncoPanel 3, FoundationOne, and TruSight Oncology 500, supplemented by AMP educational guides and the Tumor Driver Atlas. Another 172 genes were added to represent every gene in the three Cancer Hotspots releases. Panel membership explains selection; it does not establish clinical significance.

Biological comments draw on NCBI/RefSeq, UniProt, germline disease assertions, and linked literature. Cohort recurrence is distinguished from variant-specific pathogenicity. FDA references provide regulatory context and require current-label review.
