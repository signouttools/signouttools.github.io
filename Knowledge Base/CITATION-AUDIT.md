Repeated citations across different comments are counted separately. The continuing [full-text review](FULLTEXT-REVIEW.md) has restored confirmed placements and records the remaining work.

## What the audit means

This was a conservative retention audit. Automatically linked papers were not promoted to verified evidence on the basis of gene-name matching, an assertion bibliography, or cohort membership. Where specific support was not independently confirmed, the PMID was removed from report text. Removal means support was unconfirmed for that placement; it does not establish that a paper is erroneous or irrelevant to other uses. Candidate references and original evidence records are preserved.

Retained references use either a reviewed primary abstract/full-text finding or original Cancer Hotspots release rows supporting a factual observation. A citation supports its recorded subclaim, not every other assertion in a multi-claim paragraph. Database-backed statements without a verified primary PMID retain their database references. This does not certify all biological claims or clinical interpretations.

## Specific corrections

- BRCA1/BRCA2: removed breast-cohort PMID 23000897 because the inspected abstract does not establish the stated BRCA-specific somatic association. The full text gives pooled BRCA1/BRCA2 somatic counts; gene-specific supplementary confirmation remains pending.
- BRCA2: removed pancreatic-cancer PMID 25719666 from the paragraph describing breast, ovarian, and prostate cancers; the cited pancreatic finding was outside that statement.
- BRIP1, MRE11, and RAD51C: retained PMID 24240112 after checking the full-text somatic-mutation results and Figure 1C in PMC3944197, which distinguish these events from germline findings.
- SRSF2 and ZRSR2: narrowed the cited occurrence statements to myelodysplastic neoplasms. PMID 22389253 alone did not establish the broader MDS/MPN or functional-splicing wording.
- TERC: narrowed the inherited-disease statement to autosomal dominant dyskeratosis congenita and marrow failure supported by PMID 11574891.
- MCL1: retained the cross-cancer amplification/survival-dependence finding while omitting unconfirmed cancer-specific enumeration.
- BRAF V600E: retained activating-kinase evidence with a recorded historical V599E/current V600E nomenclature note.
- Hotspot study citations substantiate recorded tumor/alteration observations, not automatic pathogenicity or treatment sensitivity.
